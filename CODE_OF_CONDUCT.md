# Code of Conduct

This is an open-source research project. The goal is clear technical work and honest testing of claims.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Criticize ideas, code, and results freely and precisely.
- Assume good faith when someone reports a failure or proposes a change.
- Credit work accurately, including negative results and independent reproductions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or threats.
- Malicious disruption of the repository or its processes.
- Publishing private information about others without consent.

## Adversarial testing

Good-faith adversarial testing, challenge attempts, and careful attempts to break the Gate or verifier are **explicitly welcome**. That is part of the project’s purpose. Abuse directed at people is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close issues, reject PRs, or restrict participation that violates this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a ban from the project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies to the project’s GitHub repository and related discussion spaces used for Sovereign Veritas work.
